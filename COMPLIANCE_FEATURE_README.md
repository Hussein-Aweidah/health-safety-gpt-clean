# 📋 Compliance Gap Checker - Feature Guide

## 🎯 What is the Compliance Gap Checker?

The Compliance Gap Checker is a powerful new feature in Regis that helps businesses identify compliance gaps against New Zealand health and safety standards. It provides:

- **Industry-specific compliance templates** (Construction, Healthcare, Manufacturing, General)
- **Gap analysis** with priority scoring
- **Action planning** with timelines and resource requirements
- **Progress tracking** and compliance scoring
- **Detailed assessment interface** for ongoing compliance management

## 🚀 How to Access

1. **From Chat Interface**: Click "📋 Compliance Checker" in the sidebar
2. **From Settings**: Click "📋 Compliance Checker" button in settings page
3. **Direct Navigation**: The feature is fully integrated into the app

## 📊 Getting Started

### 1. Create Your First Assessment

1. **Click "📋 Compliance Checker"** in the sidebar
2. **Select "New Assessment"** from the mode dropdown
3. **Fill in the form**:
   - Business Name
   - Industry (Construction, Healthcare, Manufacturing, or General)
   - Assessor Name
4. **Click "Create Assessment"**

### 2. Conduct the Assessment

1. **Navigate to "Gap Analysis"** mode
2. **Review each requirement** in the assessment interface
3. **Set compliance status** for each requirement:
   - ✅ Compliant
   - ❌ Non-Compliant
   - ⚠️ Partially Compliant
   - ❓ Not Assessed
4. **Set priority levels** (Low, Medium, High, Critical)
5. **Add evidence, notes, and action items**
6. **Click "Update"** to save changes

### 3. Generate Gap Analysis

1. **Click "🔄 Generate Gap Analysis"**
2. **Review gaps by priority**:
   - 🚨 **Critical Gaps**: Immediate action required (24 hours)
   - ⚠️ **High Priority**: Action within 1 week
   - ℹ️ **Medium Priority**: Action within 1 month
   - ✅ **Low Priority**: Ongoing improvement

3. **Review the Action Plan** with:
   - Specific actions required
   - Timelines
   - Resource requirements
   - Cost estimates
   - Assignment recommendations

## 🏭 Industry Templates

### Construction Site Safety
- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**
- **Site Safety** (boundaries, signage, emergency procedures)
- **Equipment Safety** (inspections, training, maintenance)

### Healthcare Facility Safety
- **Infection Control** (hygiene, PPE, waste disposal)
- **Patient Safety** (identification, medication, fall prevention)

### Manufacturing Safety
- **Machine Safety** (guards, emergency stops, lockout procedures)
- **Chemical Safety** (SDS sheets, storage, spill response)

### General Workplace Safety
- **Workplace Environment** (lighting, ventilation, organization)
- **Employee Training** (induction, role-specific, emergency procedures)

## 📈 Understanding Your Results

### Compliance Score
- **Overall Score**: Percentage of compliant requirements
- **Category Scores**: Individual scores for each safety category
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor improvement over time

### Gap Analysis
- **Identified Gaps**: Requirements that need attention
- **Priority Levels**: Risk-based prioritization
- **Action Items**: Specific steps to achieve compliance

### Action Planning
- **Immediate Actions**: Critical gaps requiring immediate attention
- **Short-term Goals**: High-priority items (1 week)
- **Medium-term Planning**: Ongoing improvements (1 month)

## 💡 Best Practices

### Assessment Process
1. **Plan ahead** - Schedule adequate time for thorough assessment
2. **Involve stakeholders** - Include relevant team members
3. **Document everything** - Evidence, notes, and action items
4. **Set realistic timelines** - Consider resource constraints
5. **Regular reviews** - Update assessments as conditions change

### Compliance Management
1. **Prioritize critical gaps** - Address immediate safety risks first
2. **Resource allocation** - Plan for training, equipment, and process changes
3. **Communication** - Keep team informed of progress and requirements
4. **Continuous improvement** - Use assessments to drive ongoing safety culture

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **Assessment not saving**: Check all required fields are filled
- **Scores not updating**: Click "Update" button after making changes
- **Navigation issues**: Use sidebar mode selector for different views

### Data Management
- **Assessments are stored locally** in `user_data/compliance_assessments.json`
- **Backup regularly** to prevent data loss
- **Export reports** for external use or backup
